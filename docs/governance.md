# Governance Model — Orion

Governance exists to make decisions quickly and keep risk visible — not to create meetings.

## Cadence

| Forum | Frequency | Who | Purpose | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Programme Board** | Monthly | SRO, PgM, CFO rep, Compliance, Ops Dir | Funding, phase gates, red risks, scope changes | Decisions log, approved board pack |
| **Delivery review** | Fortnightly | PgM, 4 workstream leads | Cross-workstream risks, dependencies, replanning | Updated RAID & roadmap |
| **Workstream stand-up** | Weekly | Lead + squad | Progress, blockers | Blocker list |
| **Sponsor 1:1** | Weekly | SRO + PgM | Early warning, steer | Shared view before board |

## Decision gates (phase gates)

Funding is released one phase at a time. Each gate asks four questions:

1. **Did we deliver** the last phase's milestones?
2. **Is the business case still valid** (benefits & cost)?
3. **Are the risks acceptable** for the next phase?
4. **Are we ready** (resource, dependencies, compliance)?

| Gate | At | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | End of Phase 1 (M4) | Proceed to build & pilot? |
| G2 | End of Phase 2 (M9) | Proceed to national rollout? |
| G3 | End of Phase 3 (M12) | Close programme, confirm benefits, hand to BAU? |

## Escalation path

```
Blocker at squad level
      │  resolved in < 48h?  ── yes ──▶ log & move on
      │ no
      ▼
Workstream lead ── cross-stream? ──▶ Delivery review (fortnightly)
      │ no / urgent
      ▼
Programme Manager ── needs funding / scope / red risk? ──▶ Programme Board
      │ urgent & material
      ▼
SRO (out-of-cycle decision)
```

## Reporting principles
- **One source of truth:** roadmap and RAID are the master; board packs are generated from them.
- **RAG with reasons:** a colour always comes with *why* and *what we're doing about it*.
- **No surprises:** anything heading red is flagged to the SRO before the board, not at it.
- **Decisions, not updates:** every red item goes up with a recommended decision.
